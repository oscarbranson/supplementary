# Vaterite Geochemistry

This folder contains supplementary material for *Vaterite growth and trace element incorporation in the presence of Mg, Sr, B and Na.* by Lara Jurković and Oscar Branson.

## Contents

- [**00_XRD_processing.ipynb**](00_XRD_processing.ipynb): Fitting peaks to X-Ray diffraction data to determine vaterite and calcite mass ratios for seeds and precipitates.
- [**01_data_compilation.ipynb**](01_data_compilation.ipynb): Compiling geochemical and phase abundance data into a single master dataset.
- [**02_overgrwth_plots.ipynb**](02_overgrwth_plots.ipynb): Plots of overgrowth composition presented in the manuscript.
- [**03_kinetics.ipynb**](03_kinetics.ipynb): Analysis of growth kinetics, and the relevant plots in the manuscript.
- [**04_double_fractionation.ipynb**](04_double_fractionation.ipynb): Idealised models of the formation of vaterite and subsequent transformation into calcite in a biomineralisation-relevant setting.
